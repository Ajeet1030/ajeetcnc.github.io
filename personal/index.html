I'm Ajeet — most people know me as Ajeet bhai. I'm a CNC machinist based in Bhosari, Pune, and a creator by passion. Din bhar workshop ki precision duniya, aur uske baad camera aur screen par apni cinematic duniya.

I run the Instagram page @insta_wallpaper4k, where I post ultra-realistic 4K nature wallpapers every single day — roz, bina break ke.

Design se lekar launch tak, maine AttendPro LCD Edition akela banaya — ek complete attendance app with hours, overtime aur reports, premium LCD style me.

Sath me FANUC CNC programming — G-code aur drawing reading — gehrai se seekh raha hoon. Roz kuch naya, roz ek kadam aage. Growing every day.
